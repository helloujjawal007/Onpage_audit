SEO Link Checklist Rules Reference

Check A: New Tab (`target="_blank"`)

All links in the article body should open in a new tab (`target="_blank"`) to keep readers on your site. Also best practice: include `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.

Check B: Follow / Nofollow

Commercial external links must have `rel="nofollow"`. Reputable authority domains (`.org`, `.gov`, `.edu`, Wikipedia, etc.) should be dofollow (no nofollow). Configurable via reputable_domains.json.

Check C1 & C2: Internal Linking (5+ Rule)

Every blog post should link out to >= 1 relevant internal article, and published blogs must receive incoming links from at least 5 published blogs discovered via site sitemap.

Check C3: Product Page Link

Each post must link to the site's primary product page using a natural, keyword-rich anchor text (e.g. "eSignature software").

Check C4 & C5: Anchors & Duplicates

Naked links (raw URLs) and generic anchors like "here", "click here", "read more" are prohibited. No destination URL should be redundantly linked multiple times in the article body.

Check D: Keyword & KD

Integrates third-party KD providers (DataForSEO, Semrush, or transparent Offline Estimator) and inspects slug, H1, H2, 1st paragraph, and keyword density (~0.5%).
